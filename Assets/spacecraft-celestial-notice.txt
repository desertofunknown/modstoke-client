Downloaded image bytes are unchanged; filenames are changed for packaging.
The renderer applies lighting to these maps during play.

Solar System Scope combines NASA imagery with adjusted color and saturation;
unmapped areas may contain fictional terrain fill. Venus uses the atmosphere
map, not the radar surface map. These maps are representative visual imagery,
not current weather or calibrated scientific albedo data.
No endorsement by Solar System Scope, INOVE or the mirror is implied.
